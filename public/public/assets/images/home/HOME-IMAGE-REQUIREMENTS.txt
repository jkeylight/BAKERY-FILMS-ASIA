BAKERY FILMS / AI
HOME PAGE IMAGE REQUIREMENTS

PURPOSE
The homepage behaves like a film opening. Images must feel like fragments from worlds that never existed, not generic background decoration.

FOLDER
public/assets/images/home/

HEADER IMAGE NAMES
The homepage header has exactly three image slots:

1. hm-01-header-left-woman-a.webp
	Left portrait fragment beside the hero headline.

2. hm-02-header-right-city-a.webp
	Right landscape fragment near the upper headline area.

3. hm-03-header-lower-tokyo-a.webp
	Lower-right portrait fragment used during the opening transition.

Keep these three numbers fixed. Use variant b or c only for alternate crops.

NAMING SYSTEM
Use:
hm-[number]-[role]-[subject]-[variant].[extension]

Examples:
hm-00-hero-impossible-world-a.webp
hm-01-frame-woman-a.webp
hm-02-frame-city-a.webp
hm-03-frame-tokyo-a.webp
hm-04-detail-synthetic-light-a.webp
hm-05-og-film-studio-a.webp

REQUIRED HOME ASSETS

01. OPENING HERO
Filename: hm-00-hero-impossible-world-a.webp
Role: Main opening image behind the hero statement.
Recommended ratio: 2.39:1 or 16:9.
Recommended size: 2400 x 1004 minimum, or 2400 x 1350.
Subject: One cinematic world that could not be photographed in reality.
Direction: Strong atmosphere, clear focal subject, controlled negative space for text.
Avoid: Logos, readable text, busy edges, generic AI sci-fi, neon gradients.

02. OPENING FRAGMENT A
Filename: hm-01-frame-woman-a.webp
Role: Small moving fragment beside the opening headline.
Ratio: 3:4 portrait.
Recommended size: 1200 x 1600.
Subject: Fashion figure, synthetic fabric, or a person who was never photographed.
Crop: Keep the face, garment, or gesture inside the central 70 percent.

03. OPENING FRAGMENT B
Filename: hm-02-frame-city-a.webp
Role: Wide cinematic fragment during the opening sequence.
Ratio: 16:9.
Recommended size: 1600 x 900 minimum.
Subject: A city, street, or architecture that does not exist.
Crop: Leave visual breathing room around the skyline or main light source.

04. OPENING FRAGMENT C
Filename: hm-03-frame-tokyo-a.webp
Role: Secondary portrait fragment or transition image.
Ratio: 3:4 portrait.
Recommended size: 1200 x 1600.
Subject: Night, motion, fashion, or a music-video frame from an impossible time.
Crop: Preserve a strong vertical silhouette or light trail.

05. CAPABILITY PREVIEW
Filename: hm-04-detail-synthetic-light-a.webp
Role: Optional image used when hovering over What We Make.
Ratio: Flexible; 16:9 preferred.
Recommended size: 1400 x 900 minimum.
Subject: Material, product, light, fabric, or a close-up from a synthetic world.
Direction: Image must work as a cropped preview at small sizes.

06. SOCIAL SHARING IMAGE
Filename: hm-05-og-film-studio-a.webp
Role: Open Graph and social preview image.
Ratio: 1.91:1.
Size: 1200 x 630 exactly when possible.
Subject: One memorable studio image with clear negative space.
Do not place small text inside the image.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
- Preferred format: WebP.
- Use JPG only when needed for compatibility.
- Use PNG only for transparency.
- Hero target: under 1.5 MB.
- Fragment target: under 500 KB each.
- Convert all filenames to lowercase with hyphens.
- Do not use spaces, capitals, or special characters in asset filenames.
- Keep a safe central crop for mobile layouts.
- Do not upscale low-resolution images.
- Every image must have meaningful alt text when added to markup.

ALT TEXT EXAMPLES

hm-02: Wide commercial frame of a city with no real location.
hm-03: Night-time music video frame from Tokyo 2096.
hm-04: Close study of rendered light moving across synthetic material.
